    MISSION BRIEFING

    Welcome aboard, Commander. You've just been given command of the starship Space-Log. Out here a single mistake can cost the mission, so every reading, every course change and every repair has to be recorded, and you need to be able to rewind to any earlier moment.

    That's what Git is for. Think of it as the ship's flight recorder. It takes snapshots (commits) of your files, lets you test risky ideas in alternate timelines (branches) without touching the main course, and keeps a copy back at Mission Control on Earth (GitHub) through push and pull.

    Your orders: complete six missions, from powering up the recorder to sending your final log home. Nothing here touches your real computer. It's a flight simulator, so experiment freely.

    YOUR COCKPIT

    Mission Control bar
    Your route. The diamonds are the six missions. OBJECTIVE shows your current order. Stuck? Press ? HINT and the command is typed for you.
    Git Console
    Where you give orders. Type a command and press Enter. ↶ UNDO rewinds your last order.
    Commit Graph
    The star chart of your history. Each dot is a snapshot, each lane is a timeline (branch). The glowing ring is HEAD: where you are right now.
    The three zones
    Your files' journey: Working directory (on your desk) → Staging area (packed for the next snapshot) → Repository (locked into history).
    Mission Log
    Below the zones: every step of the current mission, with an explanation of why it matters. Click a command to load it.
